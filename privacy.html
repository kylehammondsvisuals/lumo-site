Privacy Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

Who we are

Lumo is operated by Kyle Hammonds Visuals LLC (d/b/a Channel X), Cleveland, Ohio. Contact: kylehammondsmedia@gmail.com.

What we collect

• Account info: your email or Apple sign-in identifier, @name, profile photo and anything you add to your profile. • Content you create: posts, comments, likes, poll votes, follows and direct messages. • Viewing activity: which episodes you watch and your progress, so you can resume. • Subscription status from Apple (via RevenueCat) — we never see your card details. • A push notification token if you turn on notifications (via OneSignal). • Basic technical data needed to run the service (device type, app version, IP address in server logs).

How we use it

To provide the app, show your profile and posts, resume playback, unlock membership content, send notifications you've turned on, keep the community safe, and respond to support requests. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for third-party advertising or tracking.

Who we share it with

Service providers that run Lumo on our behalf: Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage), RevenueCat (subscription status), OneSignal (push notifications), Vimeo (video playback) and Apple (purchases, Sign in with Apple). Your @name, photo, profile details and posts are public to other Lumo users. Direct messages are visible only to you and the recipient (and to our team if reported).

Retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. Delete your account anytime in Settings › Delete account — this permanently erases your profile, posts, messages, follows and watch history. Backups are purged within 30 days.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your information in the app, or email kylehammondsmedia@gmail.com. Lumo is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We'll post updates here and change the date above.
