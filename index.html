Manage or cancel your membership

Lumo membership is billed through your Apple ID. On your iPhone open Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Lumo. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged.

Restore your membership

In the Lumo app go to Profile › Settings › Restore purchases.

Delete your account

In the Lumo app go to Profile › Settings › Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, posts, messages and watch history.

Report content

Tap the ••• menu on any post, profile or conversation to report it or block the person. We review reports within 24 hours.
